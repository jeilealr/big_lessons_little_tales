# The Tortoise and the Hare: script (draft for owner review)

Moral: *Keep going, step by step, and you can reach places you never thought you could; and a kind friend cheers for you whether you win or lose.*

61 lines, 723 words, about 5.2 minutes at a calm storytelling pace (about 140 words a minute) before pauses. Ages 3 to 7: short sentences, repetition ("one step, and then another step"), no villain, a gentle ending where the winner is kind and the loser learns.

Speakers: NARRATOR, HATTIE (the hare), TOBY (the tortoise), OLIVE (the owl, race starter), PIP (the squirrel), BRAMBLE (the hedgehog). The line list for the narration tool is `dialogue_coverage.json` (same text, with ids, performance directions and the shots that can cover each line).

## Scene 1: Two neighbours

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Once upon a time, in a sunny meadow at the edge of the Whispering Wood, lived two neighbours who were as different as can be.  `S01-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, a little bouncy)*: Hattie the Hare was quick and bouncy. She could hop over a log before you could even say hop!  `S01-L002`
- **Hattie** *(excited, playful, full of energy, proud but friendly)*: Wheee! Look at me! I'm the fastest hopper in the whole wide wood!  `S01-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, slowing down)*: And Toby the Tortoise was slow and calm. He liked to sit in the warm sun and watch the daisies sway.  `S01-L004`
- **Toby** *(slow, calm, kind, a gentle smile in the voice)*: Good morning, sunshine. Good morning, daisies.  `S01-L005`

## Scene 2: The challenge

- **Hattie** *(bright, teasing but not mean, a little cheeky)*: Good morning, Toby! Goodness, you're slow. Do you ever actually get anywhere?  `S02-L001`
- **Toby** *(calm, steady, patient, not hurt)*: I always get where I'm going, Hattie. One step, and then another step.  `S02-L002`
- **Hattie** *(laughing lightly, playful)*: One step at a time? I can do a hundred hops in that time!  `S02-L003`
- **Hattie** *(sudden bright idea, excited)*: I know! Let's have a race! Then everyone will see who is the fastest.  `S02-L004`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Toby thought for a moment. Then he smiled a slow, steady smile.  `S02-L005`
- **Toby** *(calm, friendly, quietly confident)*: All right, Hattie. Let's race.  `S02-L006`

## Scene 3: Ready, steady, go!

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, lively)*: News of the race spread through the meadow faster than a breeze.  `S03-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Pip the Squirrel came scampering down from the trees, and Bramble the Hedgehog came trundling out of the ferns.  `S03-L002`
- **Pip** *(squeaky, excited, quick)*: A race! A race! Hattie against Toby!  `S03-L003`
- **Bramble** *(slow, cosy, a little fussy, kind)*: Oh my, oh my. I must find a good place to watch.  `S03-L004`

- **Olive** *(wise, clear, kind, like a friendly teacher)*: The race goes along the forest path, up the hill past the big shady tree, and all the way to the daisy ribbon by the old oak.  `S03-L006`
- **Olive** *(clear and exciting, building up)*: Racers, take your places. Ready... steady... go!  `S03-L007`

## Scene 8: Past the sleeping hare

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: At the top, he saw Hattie, curled up asleep under the big shady tree.  `S08-L002`

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, quiet)*: And so, very quietly, Toby walked on past.  `S08-L004`

## Scene 9: Hattie wakes up
