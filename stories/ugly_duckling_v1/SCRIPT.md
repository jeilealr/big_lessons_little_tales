# The Ugly Duckling: script (draft for owner review)

Moral: *Everyone grows in their own way and in their own time; being different is nothing to be ashamed of, and kindness to someone who feels different can help them find where they belong.*

65 lines, 990 words, about 7.7 minutes of narration (about 140 words a minute plus the pauses between lines). Ages 3 to 7: short sentences, repetition, a gentle ending.

Speakers: NARRATOR, DUCKLINGS, MAMA, OLLIE, OTTIE, SWAN. The line list for the narration tool is `dialogue_coverage.json` (same text, with ids, performance directions and the shots that can cover each line).

## Scene 1: Hatching day

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Once upon a time, beside a quiet pond on a little farm, Mama Duck sat on her nest among the tall green reeds.  `S01-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: She had been keeping her eggs warm for a long, long time, and today was a very special day.  `S01-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, playful sound words)*: Crack! Crack! Crack! One, two, three fluffy yellow ducklings popped out of their shells.  `S01-L003`
- **Mama** *(loving, delighted mother)*: Hello, my little darlings! Welcome to the world!  `S01-L004`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: But one egg was still waiting. It was bigger than the others, and a little bit speckled.  `S01-L005`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, a little suspense, gentle)*: Mama Duck waited, and waited. And then... crack!  `S01-L006`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Out tumbled a big, grey, fluffy duckling with long legs and big flat feet.  `S01-L007`
- **Mama** *(surprised, then warm and welcoming)*: Oh! Well, aren't you a big one! Welcome, little one. I shall call you Ollie.  `S01-L008`

## Scene 2: Different

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: The next morning, the ducklings looked at Ollie with big round eyes.  `S02-L001`
- **Ducklings** *(three small ducklings speaking together, curious and a little rude, not mean)*: Why are you so grey? Why are your feet so big? You don't look like us at all!  `S02-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Ollie looked at his grey feathers, and he looked at their bright yellow ones, and he felt a little lump in his throat.  `S02-L003`
- **Ollie** *(a small, soft voice, hurt and unsure)*: Maybe there's something wrong with me.  `S02-L004`
- **Mama** *(gentle, firm, loving)*: There is nothing wrong with you, Ollie. Every one of my ducklings is special in their own way.  `S02-L005`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Mama Duck tucked him under her wing, and for a little while Ollie felt warm and safe.  `S02-L006`

## Scene 3: The swimming lesson

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: That afternoon, Mama Duck led her ducklings down to the pond for their very first swim.  `S03-L001`
- **Mama** *(cheerful, encouraging teacher)*: Paddle, paddle, little ones! Kick your feet, just like me!  `S03-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: The yellow ducklings wobbled and splashed. But Ollie glided across the water as smoothly as a leaf on the breeze.  `S03-L003`
- **Mama** *(proud, delighted)*: Look at Ollie! What a wonderful swimmer!  `S03-L004`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: But when Ollie tried to quack, out came a funny honk instead. Honk!  `S03-L005`
- **Ducklings** *(giggling together, teasing)*: Honk? Ducks don't honk! You're a funny-looking, funny-sounding duck!  `S03-L006`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, tender)*: Ollie paddled away into the reeds, all by himself. Two big tears dripped off the end of his beak.  `S03-L007`

## Scene 4: Leaving the pond

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: That evening, as the sun went down, Ollie made a big decision.  `S04-L001`
- **Ollie** *(quiet, brave but sad)*: I don't fit in here. I'll go and find a place where I belong.  `S04-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: So while everyone was sleeping, Ollie waddled away along the reedy path, out into the big wide world.  `S04-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: He looked back just once, at the little pond glowing in the evening light. Then he kept on walking.  `S04-L004`

## Scene 5: The great lake

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Ollie walked for days and days, until he came to a great big lake. The leaves on the trees turned red and gold, and they floated down onto the water.  `S05-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Ollie asked the frogs if he could live with them, but frogs live in the mud. He asked the hens on a farm, but hens don't swim.  `S05-L002`
- **Ollie** *(lonely, wondering)*: Isn't there anyone like me, anywhere?  `S05-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, full of wonder)*: Then, high above him, came a sound like gentle music. A family of beautiful white swans was flying south, their long necks stretched out and their wings shining in the sun.  `S05-L004`
- **Ollie** *(amazed, longing, whispering)*: Oh... they're the most beautiful birds I've ever seen. I wish I could be like them.  `S05-L005`

## Scene 6: Winter

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Then winter came. Snow covered the ground, and the river turned to ice.  `S06-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, tender)*: Ollie was cold and hungry, and very, very lonely. He curled up in the snow by the frozen river and shivered.  `S06-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Just then, a little round face popped out of a hole in the riverbank. It was Ottie the Otter.  `S06-L003`
- **Ottie** *(kind, bubbly, warm-hearted)*: Oh, you poor thing! You're shivering! Come inside, quickly. My burrow is warm and cosy.  `S06-L004`
- **Ollie** *(shy, surprised)*: You want me to come in? Even though I'm grey, and gangly, and I honk?  `S06-L005`
- **Ottie** *(laughing kindly)*: Of course! You don't have to look like anyone else to be my friend. Come on!  `S06-L006`

## Scene 7: Friends in the snow

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: All winter long, Ollie stayed with Ottie. They shared fish soup, told stories, and slid on the ice together.  `S07-L001`
- **Ottie** *(playful, laughing)*: Wheee! Your big feet are perfect for sliding, Ollie!  `S07-L002`
- **Ollie** *(laughing, happy for the first time)*: Honk! Honk! This is the most fun I've ever had!  `S07-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: And slowly, without even noticing, Ollie grew. His neck grew longer, his wings grew stronger, and his grey feathers began to change.  `S07-L004`

## Scene 8: Spring

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: At last, spring arrived. The ice melted, the flowers bloomed, and the birds sang again.  `S08-L001`
- **Ollie** *(grateful, warm)*: Thank you, Ottie. You were my friend when I had nobody. I'll never forget it.  `S08-L002`
- **Ottie** *(warm, encouraging)*: Go and find your place, Ollie. And come back to visit!  `S08-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Ollie stretched his wings, and to his surprise they lifted him up into the sky. He was flying!  `S08-L004`

## Scene 9: The swans

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Ollie flew until he came back to the great lake, sparkling in the spring sunshine. And there, gliding on the water, were the beautiful white swans.  `S09-L001`
- **Ollie** *(nervous, small voice)*: They'll probably laugh at me, like everyone else. But I want to say hello, just once.  `S09-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: So Ollie landed softly on the water and bowed his head, waiting for them to laugh.  `S09-L003`

## Scene 10: The reflection

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, full of wonder)*: But as he bowed his head, he saw his reflection in the clear, still water.  `S10-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: He wasn't a grey, gangly duckling any more. Looking back at him was a beautiful white swan, with a long graceful neck and shining feathers.  `S10-L002`
- **Ollie** *(astonished, whispering)*: Is that... is that me?  `S10-L003`
- **Swan** *(graceful, kind, welcoming)*: Welcome, young swan! We have been waiting for you. Will you swim with us?  `S10-L004`
- **Ollie** *(joyful, emotional)*: Yes! Oh yes, I'd love to!  `S10-L005`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: For the first time, Ollie knew exactly where he belonged.  `S10-L006`

## Scene 11: Home again

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: But Ollie had not forgotten the little pond, or Mama Duck. One sunny morning, he flew home to visit.  `S11-L001`
- **Ducklings** *(amazed, together)*: Wow! Look at that beautiful swan!  `S11-L002`
- **Mama** *(recognising him, overjoyed)*: Ollie? Is that you? My Ollie!  `S11-L003`
- **Ollie** *(warm, happy)*: Hello, Mama. I'm a swan! I was a swan all along.  `S11-L004`
- **Ducklings** *(sorry, sincere, together)*: We're sorry we teased you, Ollie. We didn't understand that you were just different.  `S11-L005`
- **Ollie** *(kind, forgiving)*: That's all right. Being different isn't wrong. It just means growing in your own way.  `S11-L006`
- **Mama** *(proud, tender)*: I always knew you were special, Ollie. Not because you are a swan, but because you are you.  `S11-L007`

## Scene 12: Everyone belongs

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: From then on, Ollie spent his days gliding on the great lake with the swans, and visiting the little pond with Mama Duck and the ducklings.  `S12-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: And Ottie the Otter came to visit too, and they all splashed and played together in the sunshine.  `S12-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Because everyone grows in their own way, and in their own time.  `S12-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, the moral, slow and clear)*: And a little kindness can help anyone find the place where they belong.  `S12-L004`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, closing)*: The end.  `S12-L005`
