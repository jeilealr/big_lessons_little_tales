# The Boy Who Cried Wolf: script (draft for owner review)

Moral: *Always tell the truth, about big things and little things, so that people can believe you when it matters most; and when you make a mistake, saying sorry and telling the truth is the first step back.*

78 lines, 1078 words, about 8.5 minutes of narration (about 140 words a minute plus the pauses between lines). Ages 3 to 7: short sentences, repetition, a gentle ending.

Speakers: NARRATOR, BEN, FINN, LENA, ROSIE. The line list for the narration tool is `dialogue_coverage.json` (same text, with ids, performance directions and the shots that can cover each line).

## Scene 1: The village

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Once upon a time, in a little village tucked between green hills, lived a boy called Finn.  `S01-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Finn lived with his Grandma Rosie, who baked the warmest, softest bread in the whole village.  `S01-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Every morning the village woke up slowly. Farmer Ben fed his hens, Grandma Rosie opened her bakery, and Finn's friend Lena skipped across the square.  `S01-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: And every morning, Finn had a very important job.  `S01-L004`
- **Rosie** *(warm, loving grandmother)*: Good morning, Finn. Are you ready to look after the sheep today?  `S01-L005`
- **Finn** *(bright, eager, about eight years old)*: Yes, Grandma! I'll take them up the hill to the sweet green grass.  `S01-L006`
- **Rosie** *(warm, then serious and clear)*: Good boy. And remember: if a wolf ever comes, you shout wolf as loud as you can, and we will all come running to help.  `S01-L007`
- **Finn** *(cheerful, a promise)*: I'll remember, Grandma.  `S01-L008`

## Scene 2: On the hill

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: So Finn led the sheep up the winding path to the top of the hill. There were five fluffy sheep, and one little lamb called Daisy, who had a white heart on her nose.  `S02-L001`
- **Finn** *(counting happily, sing-song)*: One, two, three, four, five sheep... and Daisy makes six. Everybody's here!  `S02-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Daisy was Finn's favourite. She followed him everywhere and nuzzled his hand.  `S02-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, lazy and slow)*: The sheep munched the grass. Munch, munch, munch. The clouds drifted by. The sun climbed higher.  `S02-L004`
- **Finn** *(bored, sighing, a little whiny)*: Oh, this is so boring. Nothing ever happens up here.  `S02-L005`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Finn sat on the big grey rock and sighed. Then he had an idea. It was not a very good idea.  `S02-L006`
- **Finn** *(mischievous, whispering to himself)*: I know! I'll play a trick. That will make things exciting!  `S02-L007`

## Scene 3: The first trick

- **Finn** *(shouting loudly, pretending to be scared)*: Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is chasing the sheep!  `S03-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, quick)*: Down in the village, everyone stopped what they were doing, and they ran up the hill path as fast as they could.  `S03-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: But when they reached the top, there was no wolf at all. Just the sheep, munching grass, and Finn, rolling on the grass with laughter.  `S03-L003`
- **Finn** *(laughing, cheeky)*: Ha ha! There's no wolf! I tricked you!  `S03-L004`
- **Ben** *(big, deep, kind but cross)*: Finn, that's not funny. We left our work to help you.  `S03-L005`
- **Rosie** *(gentle, serious, disappointed)*: A shout for help is not a joke, Finn. Please don't do that again.  `S03-L006`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: The villagers walked back down the hill, shaking their heads.  `S03-L007`

## Scene 4: The second trick

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: The next day, Finn was on the hill again. And again, he felt bored.  `S04-L001`
- **Finn** *(mischievous, giggling)*: Last time was so funny. I'll do it one more time.  `S04-L002`
- **Finn** *(shouting, fake panic)*: Wolf! Wolf! Help, help, the wolf is here!  `S04-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Once again, the villagers ran up the hill, puffing and panting.  `S04-L004`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: And once again, there was no wolf. Only Finn, giggling behind the big grey rock.  `S04-L005`
- **Lena** *(a girl of nine, upset, hurt)*: Finn! You scared us! I thought the sheep were in danger!  `S04-L006`
- **Ben** *(slow, serious, not shouting)*: Finn, if you keep crying wolf when there is no wolf, nobody will believe you when there really is one.  `S04-L007`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, quiet)*: The villagers went home, and this time they were not just cross. They were sad.  `S04-L008`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Finn watched them go. For a moment, his tummy felt a little funny, but he shrugged and looked away.  `S04-L009`

## Scene 5: The real wolf

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: That evening, as the sun began to set and the sky turned orange and pink, something moved at the edge of the dark woods.  `S05-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, a little suspense, never scary)*: It was a wolf. A real one. He was long and grey, with a big bushy tail and a very hungry tummy.  `S05-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: The wolf crept closer, licking his lips and looking at the fluffy sheep.  `S05-L003`
- **Finn** *(frightened, a whisper)*: Oh no. Oh no! That's a real wolf!  `S05-L004`

## Scene 6: Nobody comes

- **Finn** *(shouting with all his might, truly scared, begging)*: Wolf! Wolf! Please help! There really is a wolf this time!  `S06-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Down in the village, the villagers heard Finn shouting.  `S06-L002`
- **Ben** *(tired, shaking his head)*: Not again. That boy is playing another trick.  `S06-L003`
- **Lena** *(unsure, worried)*: Should we go, Grandma Rosie?  `S06-L004`
- **Rosie** *(sad, torn)*: I don't know, Lena. I want to believe him. But he has fooled us twice.  `S06-L005`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, sad and slow)*: And so, nobody came.  `S06-L006`

## Scene 7: The bell

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, fast, never scary)*: The wolf leapt toward the flock. The sheep bleated and scattered in every direction, running into the woods to hide.  `S07-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Finn was frightened, but he was brave. He ran to the old bell on the hill and rang it with all his might.  `S07-L002`
- **Narrator** *(playful sound words, ringing and loud)*: Clang! Clang! Clang!  `S07-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, relieved)*: The loud clanging startled the wolf. He tucked his tail between his legs and ran back into the woods, far, far away.  `S07-L004`

## Scene 8: The empty hill

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, quiet)*: The wolf was gone. But so were the sheep.  `S08-L001`
- **Finn** *(calling, worried)*: Daisy? Daisy! Where are you? Where is everybody?  `S08-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, tender)*: The hill was empty and quiet. Finn sat down on the grass, and big tears rolled down his cheeks.  `S08-L003`
- **Finn** *(crying softly, sorry)*: This is all my fault. If I hadn't told those fibs, everyone would have come.  `S08-L004`

## Scene 9: The truth

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Finn ran all the way down to the village as fast as his legs could carry him.  `S09-L001`
- **Finn** *(breathless, upset, honest)*: Grandma! The wolf came, a real wolf! I rang the bell and he ran away, but the sheep ran into the woods, and Daisy is lost!  `S09-L002`
- **Ben** *(doubtful, gentle)*: Is this another trick, Finn?  `S09-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Grandma Rosie looked at Finn's face. She saw his real tears, and she knelt down beside him.  `S09-L004`
- **Finn** *(sincere, sorry, through tears)*: It's not a trick. I'm so sorry I told fibs before. I won't ever do it again. This time, it's true.  `S09-L005`
- **Rosie** *(warm, decisive, kind)*: I believe you, Finn. Come on, everyone. Let's find those sheep.  `S09-L006`

## Scene 10: The search

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: So the whole village went out to the edge of the woods, just as the first stars began to twinkle.  `S10-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: They searched behind the bushes and under the ferns, calling softly.  `S10-L002`
- **Lena** *(excited, happy)*: Here's one! And another!  `S10-L003`
- **Ben** *(pleased, deep)*: Three more over here!  `S10-L004`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: One, two, three, four, five sheep were found. But where was Daisy?  `S10-L005`
- **Finn** *(calling, hopeful)*: Daisy! Daisy, where are you?  `S10-L006`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, soft)*: Then, from under a big leafy bush, came a tiny sound. Baa.  `S10-L007`
- **Finn** *(overjoyed, relieved)*: Daisy! There you are!  `S10-L008`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Finn hugged Daisy tight, and Daisy nuzzled his cheek.  `S10-L009`

## Scene 11: A tower of blocks

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Back home, Grandma Rosie sat with Finn on the step of the little bakery, with Daisy curled up at his feet.  `S11-L001`
- **Rosie** *(wise, gentle, like a bedtime talk)*: Finn, trust is like a tower of blocks. Every time you tell the truth, you add a block, and the tower grows tall and strong.  `S11-L002`
- **Rosie** *(gentle, honest)*: But every fib knocks some blocks down. And it takes a long, long time to build the tower up again.  `S11-L003`
- **Finn** *(small voice, thoughtful)*: I knocked my tower down, didn't I?  `S11-L004`
- **Rosie** *(kind, encouraging)*: A little. But you told the truth tonight, and that was a very good block to start with.  `S11-L005`
- **Finn** *(determined, hopeful)*: Then I'll build it up again. One true word at a time.  `S11-L006`

## Scene 12: One true word at a time

- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: From that day on, Finn always told the truth, about big things and little things.  `S12-L001`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Each morning he led the sheep up the hill, and Daisy trotted right beside him.  `S12-L002`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: And if he ever felt bored, he played games that didn't fool anybody, like counting clouds, or teaching Daisy to dance.  `S12-L003`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling)*: Little by little, block by block, the villagers learned to trust Finn again.  `S12-L004`
- **Lena** *(cheerful, friendly)*: Good morning, Finn! How are the sheep today?  `S12-L005`
- **Finn** *(happy, proud, honest)*: All six of them are happy and safe. And that's the truth!  `S12-L006`
- **Narrator** *(warm, gentle storyteller for small children, unhurried, smiling, the moral, slow and clear)*: Because when you always tell the truth, people will believe you when it matters most.  `S12-L007`
